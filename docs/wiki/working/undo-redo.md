# Undo and redo

Every structural change can be undone.

## Shortcuts

- **Undo**: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>
- **Redo**: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>

They are also in the **Edit** menu and the toolbar (↶ / ↷).

## What is tracked

Undo and redo cover **structural** changes: adding and deleting nodes and edges, reassigning sections, locking, setting dates, and adding comments.

Pure view changes are not on the history stack: node positions and [Preferences](/getting-started/preferences) are saved directly, so they are not undone with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>. Use **⟳ Reset** to clear positions back to the computed layout.
